A key role of *Convergence* is to showcase the ideas and perspectives of leaders and innovators in and outside campus. This April issue features a conversation with former Minister of Environment and Climate Change Steven Guilbeault and an exclusive interview with Year 11 student Adam Zhu about his AI-based Community Economic Development project, along with the successful endeavours of various UCC co-curricular and extra-curricular organizations.
