Toronto is in the midst of a nail-bitingly close and sharply polarizing municipal election campaign. With that in mind, this issue of *Convergence* is a school-focused preview of the election. The issue features a breakdown of the results of our poll from October 5-8, as well as the return of UCC's best humour and satire publication, the *Blazer*, with its emphatic endorsement of Edward (Xiaohua) Gong.
